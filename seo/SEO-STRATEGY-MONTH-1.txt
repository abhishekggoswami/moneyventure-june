2. WHY THE "KEYWORD STUFFING" APPROACH IS AVOIDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A common misconception is that placing hundreds or thousands of keywords into
a web page (or hiding them in the code) makes a site rank higher. The opposite
is true.

  • Google STOPPED using the old "meta keywords" tag for ranking in 2009.
  • Cramming keywords into visible or hidden text is called "keyword stuffing"
    and is an explicit violation of Google's Spam Policies.
  • Sites caught doing this are DEMOTED or REMOVED from search results entirely.

For a SEBI-registered financial business, ranking integrity and trust are
critical. A penalty would damage both rankings and reputation. Therefore our
1,500 keywords are used as a RESEARCH AND CONTENT-PLANNING asset — they guide
what pages, headings, and articles we create — rather than being dumped into
the code.

         (helps Google show rich, trusted business information)
  [DONE] Mobile-friendly, fast-loading pages (Core Web Vitals friendly)
  [DONE] Semantic HTML headings and descriptive image alt text

These are the foundations Google rewards — they directly support ranking
without any risk of penalty.

6. PENALTY-SAFE / WHITE-HAT GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every technique used in this campaign follows Google's official Search
Essentials (Webmaster Guidelines). Specifically:

  WE DO                                    WE DO NOT
  --------------------------------------   ----------------------------------
  Write helpful, original content          Stuff keywords into pages
  Use keywords naturally in real copy      Hide text or links
  Add proper titles & descriptions         Use the obsolete meta-keywords trick
  Build structured data / schema           Buy spam backlinks
  Improve site speed & mobile UX           Create doorway / duplicate pages
  Target genuine search intent             Cloak content from Google

This ensures the client's site grows safely and the rankings, once earned,
are durable.
