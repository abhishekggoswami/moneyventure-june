C. STRUCTURED DATA (SCHEMA.ORG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A "FinancialService" schema block was added. This is invisible to visitors but
tells Google, in a machine-readable way:

  • Business name: Money Ventures Research
  • Type: SEBI Registered Research Analyst (INH000026114)
  • Services: Equity, Intraday, Options, Commodity research, IPO advisory
  • Area served: India
  • Areas of expertise: technical analysis, investment advisory, etc.

This improves how the business appears in search and builds trust signals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. HOW TO VERIFY THE WORK (FOR THE CLIENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Visit  your-domain.com/sitemap.xml  — you will see all pages listed.
  2. Visit  your-domain.com/robots.txt   — you will see crawl rules + sitemap.
  3. Right-click any page > "View Page Source" and search for:
        - <title>            (unique title)
        - "description"      (unique meta description)
        - "application/ld+json"  (structured data block)
  4. Paste any page URL into Google's Rich Results Test
     (search.google.com/test/rich-results) to confirm valid structured data.
